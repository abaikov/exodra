Escape hatches

Two things most frameworks forbid, running at once: a list where ONE child is written in vanilla JS, and a subtree owned by a renderer that is not Exodra at all. Both arrive through exoForeignNode, and Exodra places, moves and disposes them like any other child.

What the page is built to show: every line the log prints is produced by a hook; the first mount reports in the document: false because an instance is armed and inserted afterwards; both unmounts fire while their elements are still attached; a re-mount reports true; and the canvas resumes its frame count instead of restarting it. Moving either foreign thing leaves mounts at 1 — a move is not a remount. exoForeignNode takes the exonode first — exoForeignNode(c, element, hooks?) — because it reads the schema off it, which is why the foreign children are built inside createInitialState: there is no exonode at module scope.
