Two hundred rows, the same component, the same handler, rendered twice. The left panel gets the shipped strategy — one addEventListener per row. The right one composes a delegating pack over the same seam — one listener on the render root, and the rows carry their handlers as properties. Both panels answer clicks; the counter under each is written by a real handler.
